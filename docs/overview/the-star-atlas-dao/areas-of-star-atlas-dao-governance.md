## [The Star Atlas DAO — The Game of POLIS](https://medium.com/star-atlas/the-star-atlas-dao-the-game-of-polis-fd3798aa6ebe)

As the DAO matures, members will have the power to govern over a wide range of issues, activities, and development. These include:

* Ecosystem development and interoperability
* This could include everything from potential partnerships and integrations to game expansions
* Metaverse economics
* Power to set rewards, incentives, and rates of taxation
* Treasury management (see below)
* Game system design
* In-game law establishment

Eventually, members will also get to vote on things like the rules regulating lower-tier DAOs, protocol upgrades, and emergency actions.
