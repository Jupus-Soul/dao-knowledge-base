> The DAO’s treasury is managed by the DAO through on-chain governance mechanisms. Any funds that the DAO decides to send off-chain may be deployed by entities such as the Star Atlas Foundation, but only after receiving on-chain approval by the DAO and subject to the direction of the DAO. The DAO’s treasury is funded by proceeds from what amounts to taxation on transactions in the ecosystem, consistent with the parameters set forth in the [Star Atlas Economics Paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/sa-cdn-prod/dao/economy/reports/star-atlas-economics-paper.pdf).
>
> *—*[*The Star Atlas DAO Releases its Sustainable Governance Framework*](https://medium.com/star-atlas/the-star-atlas-dao-releases-its-sustainable-governance-framework-5a45b4fad790)

## Income Streams

The ATMTA team has kindly created several income streams for the DAO, to seed its treasury. This initially started with R4 Sales.

### Old Streams

The following income streams are no longer available:

#### 📈 Team R4 Sales

100% of primary R4 sales — Until SAGE Labs rolled out, Faction Fleet (SCORE) players had to purchase R4 (in **ATLAS**) to upkeep their staked ship. The team offered R4 sales on their Galactic Marketplace using the [NPC public key](https://solscan.io/account/NPCxfjPxh6pvRJbGbWZjxfkqWfGBvKkqPbtiJar3mom). All proceeds were periodically swept to the primary DAO treasury.

##### Active Period

This was active between **December 16th, 2021—**&#x63;oinciding with the launch of the SCORE—and **June 8th, 2023—**&#x63;oinciding with a big boost in Claim Stake & Escape Velocity output + shuttering of primary R4 sales.

##### References

* End: [Claim Your Destiny: The Dawn of the Player-Driven Star Atlas Economy](https://staratlas.com/newsroom/star-atlas-news/claim-your-destiny-the-dawn-of-the-player-driven-star-atlas-economy#)

### Current Streams

The following income streams are currently active:

#### 📈 Marketplace Fees

33% of royalty fees in the Star Atlas Galactic Marketplace — Galactic Marketplace fees for each transaction are transferred to a [fees account](https://solscan.io/account/feesQYAaH3wjGUUQYD959mmi5pY8HSz3F5C3SVc1fp3) as part of the transaction. This account is periodically swept, with one-third of the balance sent to the DAO treasury account and the rest sent to ATMTA.

##### Active Period

This has been active since **September 29th, 2022**, coinciding with the launch of the ATLAS Locker.

##### References

* Start: [Star Atlas DAO Releases ATLAS Locker](https://staratlas.com/newsroom/star-atlas-news/star-atlas-dao-releases-atlas-locker)

#### 📈 IP Project Royalty Fees

Once an IP licensee generates more than $1 million USD in lifetime Gross Revenue with their project, they will need to start paying Royalty Rates for any Gross Revenue beyond the $1 million USD exclusion. Of that fee, 5% will go to the DAO.

Note that no community project has reached this stage yet.

##### Active Period

This has been active since **April 26, 2023**, coinciding with the launch of the IP Program.

100% of crafting fees from Star Atlas Golden Era (SAGE) game mode.

## Expense Stream

### Foundation - $100k - Annually

As per[ PIP-2](../archive/foundational-pips/pip-2-star-atlas-foundation.md), the DAO sets aside an annual budget of (up to) $100,000 USD from its treasury for the operations and maintenance of the Star Atlas Foundation, including director fees—supervisor, registered office, and secretary services—and other expenses (including legal fees and other services necessary for operations).

Any further costs beyond basic operations and maintenance, including to retain additional service providers, shall require on-chain approval by the Star Atlas DAO in advance.

## Ecosystem Fund

Through [PIP-4](../archive/foundational-pips/pip-4-star-atlas-ecosystem-fund.md), the DAO established a fund and process for community members to apply for grants to develop various aspects of Star Atlas. It was seeded with a budget of 20% of the Star Atlas DAO’s ATLAS treasury.

Some examples of efforts that could be funded under this proposal include:

* Creating software tools that enhance player experiences
* Hosting community in-person or virtual meetups
* Supporting educational or informational initiatives to grow the Star Atlas community
* Building new creative works or materials — such as videos, art, or music — based on Star Atlas intellectual property

Other types of efforts could be funded as well, as long as they relate to the development or improvement of Star Atlas, and as long as the product or experience is open to all Star Atlas community members (rather than, for example, benefiting only one guild or faction).
