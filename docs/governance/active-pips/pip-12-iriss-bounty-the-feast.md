---

## **Proposal Details:**

1. **Eligibility:** Any Star Atlas community member is eligible to apply for funding.
2. **Funding Cap:** A total of $10,000 of Atlas will be allocated, with a **maximum of $500** per request, calculated at the time of dispersal. Funds will be dispersed in Atlas after all the proof of event documentation has been provided. 
3. **Required Application Details:**
   * Brief description of the event (e.g., type of gathering, location, plan).
   * Expected number of guests.
   * Estimated costs.
   * Solana Wallet for disbursement
4. **Event Requirements:**
   * The event must be sufficiently advertised.
   * Must be a public event and open to any persons with a connection to Star Atlas.
   * The event should be documented with photos and videos that will be shared with the Star Atlas DAO Council. All content may be shared with the Star Atlas team and the community for promotional use.

## How to apply:

To apply for reimbursement under the "Iris's Bounty: The Feast" proposal, please fill out the form at this link. A council member will contact you to provide feedback or confirm pre-approval for your event.
