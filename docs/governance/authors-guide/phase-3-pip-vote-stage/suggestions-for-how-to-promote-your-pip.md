title: "Suggestions for how to Promote Your PIP"
---

**Self Promoting**

*  PIP author can request time in the brew from Jose
*  Reach out to larger PVP holders by asking in #dao-chat channel on SA discord
* Reach out to DACs in their discords–find them in #guilds channel in SA discord, and DAC registry on SA website

\
Engage Respectfully

* Treat the fourteen days as an opportunity to campaign and persuade–avoid attacking back
* Engage in #dao-chat and highlight what your PIP offers the DAO– ROI/impressions/views/etc
* Answer questions as if talking to everyone watching the chat–not just the person who asked you
* The vote isn’t done until the time expires

How to use Social Media and Discord

* Post on socials
*  Talk in #dao-chat
* Post updates in your PIP voting thread

\
Language Barrier: Most of the ecosystem conducts DAO business in English. If English is difficult for you:

* Use a translator service to translate to your language
* Use AI to decipher acronyms or subtext that a translator won’t pick up
* Ask an English speaker to assist.
