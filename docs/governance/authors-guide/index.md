## Overview

The PIP process takes place primarily in the Star Atlas Discord server, within the dedicated DAO section. A successful PIP moves through three distinct phases before implementation:

1. **Ideation Phase** - Initial concept development and community feedback
2. **Draft Phase** - Formal proposal writing and refinement
3. **Voting Phase** - Community decision through governance framework voting

### Getting Started

Before submitting your first PIP, ensure you:

* Have access to the Star Atlas Discord server \[[Star Atlas Discord](https://discord.gg/F2GNAAVxXS)]
* Are familiar with the DAO section channels \[[dao-chat](https://discord.com/channels/789226204980707409/1026590874865504426)]
* Understand the current needs and priorities of the Star Atlas ecosystem

### The Three Phases Explained

#### Phase 1: PIP Ideation

During this initial phase, authors present their ideas to the community for early feedback and discussion. This helps identify potential issues and gauge community interest before investing time in a full proposal.

#### Phase 2: PIP Draft

Once an idea has received positive feedback, authors develop a comprehensive draft following the standard [PIP template.](../creating-a-proposal/index.md) This draft undergoes community review and council revision.

#### Phase 3: PIP Vote

After the draft is finalized, the council will recommend approval from the foundation, and the proposal moves to an official governance vote where DAO members can cast their votes to approve or reject the PIP.

For a more in-depth view of the PIP process, check [The Legislative Process](../the-legislative-process/index.md)
