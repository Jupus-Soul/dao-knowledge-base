## Star Atlas DAO Council

> ℹ️ The fourth term officially started on October 11th, 2026\
> It will end 9 months later, on July 11th, 2027

### Overview

The fourth term of the Star Atlas DAO Council runs from October 11, 2026 to July 11, 2027. The Council was elected through [PIP-35 (Council Election #4)](https://govern.staratlas.com/proposals/7fb06b04-1158-4da3-af8e-dd4c60b973ce), following the election process set out in [PIP-10](https://govern.staratlas.com/proposals/ed887a4e-344e-44d6-8a47-c815d9e8ec8e).

Three candidates nominated themselves for the five available seats. Because the number of candidates did not exceed the number of available seats, all three candidates were elected.

### Elected Council Members

* **Emperor** (emperoraa)\*
* **jith blade** (jithblade)\*
* **Earther** (Earther \[ROME])

Two of the five Council seats were not filled in this election.

### The Election

* **Proposal:** [PIP-35 (Council Election #4)](https://govern.staratlas.com/proposals/7fb06b04-1158-4da3-af8e-dd4c60b973ce)
* **Voting opened:** August 30, 2026
* **Voting closed:** September 13, 2026
* **Seats available:** 5
* **Nominees:** 3

> ℹ️ Because this election had only three nominees for five available seats, STV did not operate as it ordinarily would in a contested election. All three nominees were elected; the ballot was a procedural formality that recorded participation and the outcome in the official DAO governance system.
