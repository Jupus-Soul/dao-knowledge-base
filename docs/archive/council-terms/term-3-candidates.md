### 2. \[ECL] YassFreeks

* **Discord Username:** atlas\_freeks
* **X Profile:** [Freeks Code (@FreeksCode) / X](https://x.com/FreeksCode)
* **Current PVP**: 18 452

> Member of the French guild ECL Eclypse, active in the Star Atlas ecosystem for over three years. Passionate about SAGE Labs, resource management, and the economic balance of the metaverse.\
> I strongly support community-driven initiatives like the Arty Hosting Service, which helps small players access affordable automation hosting to grow their fleets.\
> My goal is to represent builders and everyday players who make Star Atlas thrive — by supporting practical, scalable solutions that strengthen both the economy and inclusiveness of the DAO.

#### 1)  Why are you interested in serving on the Star Atlas DAO Council? 

I’m deeply passionate about the long-term vision of Star Atlas — not only as a game, but as a pioneering metaverse economy. I want to contribute to building systems that empower players, guilds, and creators to thrive within this universe. Joining the DAO Council is an opportunity to represent the community’s voice with clarity, transparency, and purpose. I believe in practical governance — balancing innovation with sustainability — and ensuring that every decision helps Star Atlas grow into the most immersive and self-sustaining Web3 ecosystem in gaming.

#### 2) What are you most excited to work on as a Council member?

I’m most excited to help shape the framework for player-driven governance, economic balancing, and guild incentives. I want to strengthen the bridge between developers and the community — making feedback loops more efficient, data-driven, and transparent. I’m also eager to collaborate on proposals that improve resource flow, in-game industries, and the overall sustainability of the Star Atlas economy. In short, I want to help Star Atlas evolve into a thriving, player-owned galaxy that rewards creativity, strategy, and collaboration.

#### 3) What adjustments do you think should be made to the DAO structure/organization to enhance its effectiveness?

The DAO needs a clearer operational framework that allows faster execution of community-driven initiatives. I believe improving transparency, accountability, and communication between the Council, Working Groups, and builders would significantly enhance efficiency.\
We should prioritize:

Better coordination tools and dashboards (like a “Zink”-style governance dashboard) for proposal tracking and resource allocation.

Simplifying participation for smaller guilds and solo players, making governance more inclusive.

Defining clearer milestones aligned with the project’s roadmap — CORE, Starpath, Never Alone, Holosim, and UE Patch — to ensure DAO actions are synchronized with development progress.\
Ultimately, a DAO should operate with the same agility as a live ecosystem — structured enough to be reliable, but flexible enough to evolve with the community.

#### 4) Describe your relevant experience with gaming, governance and DAOs.

I’ve been part of the Star Atlas universe for over three years, actively following its growth and engaging with its evolving economy and gameplay. As a dedicated SAGE Labs player since launch, I’ve developed a strong understanding of crafting systems, resource logistics, and economic strategy within the game.\
I’m also a proud member of ECL Eclypse, a French guild focused on teamwork, learning, and contribution to the broader Star Atlas community. My goal is to help the guild grow in reputation while supporting a healthier and more connected ecosystem.\
Having experienced multiple phases of the project — from CORE to Holosim — I’m excited to contribute to shaping a DAO that reflects the creativity, strategy, and passion of its community.

#### 5) Which parts of Star Atlas are you participating in?

* POLIS Locker
* SAGE
* UE5

#### 6) Please disclose any potential conflicts of interest you might have in the gaming sector/web3.

I have no conflicts of interest in the gaming or Web3 sectors. My only focus is on contributing to the growth of Star Atlas and its community.
