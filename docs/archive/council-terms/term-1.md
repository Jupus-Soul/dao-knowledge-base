## Star Atlas DAO Council - First Term

> ℹ️ The first term officially started on October 11th, 2024, at 16:00 UTC\
> It ended precisely 6 months later, on April 11th, 2025, at 16:00 UTC

### Overview

The first-ever Star Atlas DAO Council term ran from October 11, 2024, at 16:00 UTC, until April 11, 2025. This inaugural council established foundational governance processes for the Star Atlas DAO.

## Election Process

The first Star Atlas DAO Council election consisted of two rounds, incorporating, for the first time, the Single Transferable Vote (STV) system to redistribute excess votes:

1. **First Round**: 22 candidates participated, with the top 12 advancing to the second round.
2. **Second Round**: The final 5 council members were elected from these 12 candidates.
